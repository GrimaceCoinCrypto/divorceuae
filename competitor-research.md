## 6. PROPERTY DIVISION KEY FACTS
- UAE has **NO community property concept** — assets owned by title only
- Who paid for property is irrelevant unless a "contribution claim" is filed under civil law
- **Offshore assets / corporate-held property:** disclosure obligations apply; forensic tracing may be needed
- **Mahr (dower) types:**
  - Mu'ajjal = prompt mahr (paid at marriage)
  - Mu'ajjall = deferred mahr (due at divorce or death)
  - Enforced via Execution Court as a legal debt
- **GPSSA pensions** (government employees): inalienable — cannot be divided
- **Private pensions:** valued by actuary, subject to agreement
- **Pre-divorce gift transfer vs. post-divorce transfer:** 0.125% vs. 4% DLD fee — huge financial difference. Transfer property BEFORE divorce is finalized if both parties agree.
